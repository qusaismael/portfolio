### Composition

In baseline Work at 1440px, repeated filled/bordered project cards give photographs, evidence screenshots, and prose similar visual weight. The paper media frames are conspicuous on dark mode. Proposed Gate A treatment: evidence-led images with open text underneath, preserving every title, caption, link, tag, and order. This is a proposal, not approved yet.

### Typography and reading

Home retains its voice and real photographs; article length is not a defect. Assess proposed reading type and section rhythm by opening before/after captures at full or cropped resolution, not just compressed full-page thumbnails.

### Mobile ergonomics

At 390px the closed baseline header uses two rows (brand/location above, theme/Menu below). Personal Life previews reduce imagery to narrow strips and clamp the descriptions. At 320px one hero CTA wraps to a second row; do not hide it or force it into tiny text merely to fit. Proposed Gate A: compact header with full location/time and image-led, complete-description Life cards. Taller cards are intentional.

### Image treatment

The 390px Life photographs have narrow crops, and their overlay captions are hidden. Baseline Work screenshots use contain, while personal photo cards use cover; inspect legibility and faces at full size before changing media treatment. A capture with no broken images is not a transfer/performance score.

### Dark and light parity

Both themes have full baseline captures. Before approving a design, compare identical route/width/state, verify control/focus contrast and light-theme image mats, and check geometry does not jump on theme change.

### Personality retained

Preserve Qusai's authored prose and section order, Arabic name, photographs, wandering cat, terminal, annotations, and Jordan/time. `npm run test:site` protects baseline strings/order; review with a human as well.

### Specific changes requested by the owner

UI/UX quality is the priority. Gate A must show clear visual gains on Home, Work, and article at desktop/mobile in both themes **before** propagating changes to interior pages. No changes have been deployed; owner review pending.

## After / Gate A findings — owner approval pending

The after set is `preview/after/` (24 PNGs and 24 matching metric records); the interaction samples are in `preview/states/`. Built output, not the live domain. `npm run build`, `npm test`, `npm run test:site`, and all ten design browser tests plus the smoke test passed. The capture recorded **zero horizontal overflow, broken loaded images, and uncaught page errors** at all 24 route/theme/viewport combinations. Every metric height matches its PNG, with no dark/light height mismatch at a paired width. This does not measure Lighthouse, network transfers, or every interactive state.

### Composition

On Work at 1440px, the old filled/shadowed card shells are now open entries: evidence is framed above text and each entry ends on a quiet rule. The dark/light palette, ruled writing list, and page headings remain. Compare [old Work dark 1440](before/sites-1440-dark.png) with [new Work dark 1440](after/sites-1440-dark.png); the 390px counterpart is [old](before/sites-390-light.png) versus [new](after/sites-390-light.png). The page still shows selected projects before the small tools, in the authored order.

### Typography and reading

The archived article body is 18px with a 1.8 line-height and a bounded measure; Work metadata is at least 13px. At 390px, the article grows from 4,855px to 5,332px, rather than hiding text or squeezing lines. Compare [old article](before/writing-tracing-a-kernel-panic-what-actually-happens-when-you-hit-sleep-6e1e2bffa4bd-390-dark.png) with [new article](after/writing-tracing-a-kernel-panic-what-actually-happens-when-you-hit-sleep-6e1e2bffa4bd-390-dark.png). Its original words and sequence remain.

### Mobile ergonomics

At 390px, the closed header now puts QI, the theme switch and Menu on one row, and Jordan/time on the second; the menu opens to complete, 48px-tall destinations. At 320px the location label remains within the viewport; the focus ring is visible in both themes and Escape returns focus to Menu. See [dark open menu](states/menu-390-dark.png) and [light focused control](states/focus-390-light.png). Life previews now show complete descriptions and captions instead of one-line truncation. Home height at 390px grows **7,249 → 8,409px** (+1,160px), primarily because the authentic square photographs are shown full width. That extra scroll is a deliberate tradeoff to judge, not a speed gain. Compare [old Home](before/home-390-light.png) with [new Home](after/home-390-light.png).

### Image treatment

The two square Life photographs keep their full frames on mobile; the people and captions are visible. Work photographs and interface screenshots use `object-fit: contain`; all nine Work previews loaded in the 1440/390 browser test. The smaller human photographs sit inside theme-aware matte on Work, leaving side space but avoiding cropped people. The light screenshot matte remains warm paper. Project detail evidence now opens in the existing native image dialog, with its original image URL as a fallback link.

### Dark and light parity

Matched captures exist at 1440, 1024, 390 and 320 in both themes. The captured document geometry matches between dark/light for every paired route/width; focus/menu states were inspected at 390 in both themes. The clock and optional external-feed contents can vary between capture times, so these are composition comparisons rather than pixel-perfect diffs.

### Personality retained

The cat, terminal, name jokes and Arabic note, Jordan clock, real presentation/travel photographs, candid project limitations, and GPG/contact remain. `npm run test:site` passed the authored-data and homepage-order assertions. The photo captions and prose were not rewritten; selected work still follows experience, not an invented product-marketing order.

In particular: approve or reject the taller full-photo mobile Life cards, the open Work entries (including the photo side matte), and the compact two-row header.
