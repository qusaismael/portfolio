How I got here

I work across security, privacy, and software. Sometimes that means writing policies. Sometimes it means hardening a Linux server or getting an AI model to run without sending data anywhere.

I have always wanted to know HOW things work. My dad is a computer engineer, which meant a lot of my questions ended with a computer taken apart, or an operating system being reinstalled. That explains a lot, actually.

I still like learning that way. And I love being in rooms where everyone thinks a little differently. Open source gives me a lot of those rooms.

I'm based in Jordan. When I'm not working, there are games, places to explore, and a slightly unreasonable interest in my computer setup.
